FFR-1 · reduced-order model

Spread over a thin sheet is set by how fast heat conducted forward from the flame raises the fuel to its pyrolysis temperature (de Ris). Thick fuels add the flow, because the gas-phase length scale sets how deep the heat reaches. FFR-1 blends the two and multiplies by two loss factors.

V_thin = (π/4) · λg (Tf − Tv) / (ρs cs τ (Tv − T∞)) V_thick = U · (ρ λ c)g (Tf − Tv)² / ((ρ λ c)s (Tv − T∞)²) V = √(V_thin² + V_thick²) · (1 − 1/Da) · (1 − χ) U = (U_forced³ + U_buoyant³)^⅓, U_buoyant = (g αg ΔT / T∞)^⅓ Da = (αg/U²) · A P² Y_O2 · exp(−Ta/Tf) blowoff when Da ≤ 1 χ = ε σ (Tv⁴ − T∞⁴) / (λg (Tf − Tv) U / αg) quench when χ ≥ 1 Tf = T∞ + k_Tf · Y_O2 k_Tf calibrated on 1 g data

FFR-2D · detailed solver

A transient 2D finite-difference solution of the gas above the sheet, coupled to the sheet itself. Nothing about the spread rate is assumed: the flame ignites, heats the sheet ahead of it, and moves. The front position is tracked and fitted, as in the BASS-II image analysis.

∂T/∂t − U ∂T/∂x = α∇²T + (Q/cp) ω − 4σκ(T⁴ − T∞⁴)/(ρ cp) ∂Yf/∂t − U ∂Yf/∂x = α∇²Yf − ω ∂Yo/∂t − U ∂Yo/∂x = α∇²Yo − s ω ω = B ρ Yf Yo exp(−Ta/T) one-step, second order Sheet (per face, half-thickness τ): m (cs dTs/dt) = λ ∂T/∂y|wall − εσ(Ts⁴ − T∞⁴) − ṁ″ Lv ṁ″ = m · As exp(−Es/Ts), dm/dt = −ṁ″, fuel enters the gas at ṁ″

Grid 0.5 mm over 40 × 12 mm, explicit time step about 0.25 ms. Constant density, plug flow, unity Lewis number. Buoyancy enters only as an added plug-flow velocity.
